Community Guidelines (Platform Rules)

Last updated: 06.04.2026

These Community Guidelines explain what is allowed and not allowed on pusto.sk. By using the platform, you agree to follow these rules.

1. Purpose of the platform

pusto.sk is a marketplace and discovery platform where users can find and publish listings for goods and services, jobs, accommodation, and roommates. We aim to keep the platform safe, transparent, and useful for everyone.

2. General principles

Users must provide accurate and truthful information, communicate respectfully, comply with applicable laws, and act in good faith.

3. Prohibited content

The following content is strictly prohibited: illegal or harmful content such as illegal goods or services, drugs or controlled substances, stolen items, and weapons where prohibited by law. Fraud and scams including fake listings, misleading prices or conditions, phishing or attempts to steal money or data, and impersonation of another person or company. Unsafe or exploitative content including exploitative housing offers, discriminatory job listings, harassment or hate speech. Spam and abuse such as duplicate listings, mass posting, irrelevant or misleading categories, and manipulation of rankings or fake engagement. Privacy violations including publishing personal data without consent, doxxing or exposing private information. Technical abuse such as malware or malicious links, attempts to hack, scrape, or disrupt the platform.

4. Listing requirements

All listings must be accurate and up to date, clearly describe the offer, include real price and conditions, and not mislead users. We may remove or edit listings that are incomplete, misleading, or violate these rules.

5. User responsibility

Users are solely responsible for the content they publish, communication with other users, and any transactions or agreements. pusto.sk does not guarantee the accuracy of listings, successful transactions, or the identity of users. Always verify information before making payments or agreements.

6. Safety recommendations

To stay safe: do not send money in advance without verification, meet in public places when possible, avoid sharing sensitive personal information, and report suspicious behavior.

7. Reporting violations

If you see content that violates these rules, use the report function on the platform or contact us at: pusto.sk.com@gmail.com. Please include link or description of the content and the reason for the report.

8. Enforcement

We may take actions including removing listings, limiting visibility, requesting edits, suspending or banning accounts. Actions are taken to protect users and maintain platform integrity.

9. Repeated violations

Users who repeatedly violate these rules may be temporarily restricted or permanently banned.

10. Updates

We may update these Guidelines from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page. By using pusto.sk, you agree to follow these Community Guidelines.
